Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our woodwork services.

What types of woodwork services do you offer?

We offer custom furniture, kitchen cabinets, wardrobes, wooden doors, office furniture, and complete interior woodwork including TV consoles, wall paneling, and decorative elements.

How do I get a quote for my project?

Simply message us on WhatsApp at 0303 3939167 with your requirements. We'll discuss your project, take measurements if needed, and provide you with a transparent quotation.

How long does a typical project take?

Timeline depends on the scope of work. Small items like a wardrobe may take 1-2 weeks, while complete kitchen or interior projects may take 3-6 weeks. We'll give you a clear timeline with the quote.

Can you design furniture according to my specific needs?

Absolutely! Custom design is our specialty. Share your ideas, dimensions, and any reference images, and we'll create furniture that fits your space and style perfectly.

Where is A.R. Woodworks located?

We are located in Federal B Area FC Area, Karachi, Pakistan. We serve clients across Karachi and can arrange delivery and installation at your location.

Do you provide delivery and installation?

Yes! We provide delivery and professional installation of all furniture. Our team ensures everything is set up perfectly at your location.
